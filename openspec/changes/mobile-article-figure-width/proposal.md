# Mobile article figure width

On mobile, article figures inherit the browser's default 40px side margins. Those margins narrow charts and other figure content on top of the reader's intentional text padding, making embedded labels harder to read.

Remove the extra figure margins in the mobile reading view while preserving the article's normal horizontal padding and the desktop layout.

## Scope

- Reset horizontal margins on article figures at the mobile breakpoint.
- Keep text padding and desktop figure spacing unchanged.
