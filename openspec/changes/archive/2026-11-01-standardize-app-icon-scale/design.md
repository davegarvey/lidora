# Design

Use 16 px for the icon that serves as an action control's visual cue, whether it appears alone or alongside a short label. This applies across navigation, toolbars, rows, settings, forms, dialogs and mobile controls. Use 14 px for inline link adornments, input hints, drag handles, sort-direction hints and status spinners. Use 12 px for compact inline affordances and tiny status markers. Noninteractive swipe feedback may use 22 px. Keep interactive hit areas independent; use at least 44 × 44 px for coarse-pointer controls.

The custom Lidora brand mark remains governed by its own dimensions.
