# Increase sidebar heading action touch targets

Give the mobile Add and Refresh actions in the sidebar more room for touch input while preserving their compact icons.

## Why

The sidebar Add and Refresh buttons currently have 36 px targets on touch devices, smaller than the 44 px targets used by the top bar, river header and reader action bar.

## What changes

- Increase the mobile/coarse-pointer hit areas for the sidebar heading actions to 44 × 44 px.
- Keep the Lucide icons at 16 px and preserve the existing desktop button dimensions.

## Non-goals

- Changing feed tag chip sizes or sidebar layout.
