# Quieter reading chrome

Keep the reading pane's top bar to controls that act on the open article.

## Why

The reading chrome mixed three kinds of element. The star was always Mauve, although Mauve is reserved for unread and selected states, so an unstarred article looked starred. The keyboard-shortcuts button gave permanent prominence to a rarely used control that is unrelated to the article and already available from the sidebar, Settings and the `?` key. The library activity icon and pending Show action appeared and disappeared inside the button row, moving the Focus mode control and the chrome title.

## What changes

- The star uses the neutral icon colour until the article is starred, then Mauve.
- The reading chrome no longer shows a keyboard-shortcuts button.
- The reading chrome no longer shows library activity or the pending Show action. A visible sidebar or collapsed rail shows them while reading; otherwise they appear on return to the list.

## Non-goals

- Changing when library activity runs or when pending results are held.
- Changing the mobile bottom action bar's contents.
