## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Active toggle color is preserved on hover

When a river-header toggle is active, pointer hover SHALL preserve its active accent icon color while applying the existing hover background.

#### Scenario: Hover over an active filter
- **WHEN** the pointer hovers over an active Starred or Unread toggle
- **THEN** the icon SHALL retain the active accent color
- **AND** the hover background SHALL remain visible

#### Scenario: Hover over active For you ordering
- **WHEN** the pointer hovers over the active For you toggle
- **THEN** the icon SHALL retain the active accent color
- **AND** the hover background SHALL remain visible
