# Preserve active toggle colors on hover

Keep active river-header filter and order icons visibly active when the pointer moves over them.

## Why

The general hover color overrides the active icon color, making a selected filter look inactive while hovered.

## What changes

- Preserve the active accent color on hover for Starred, Unread and For you header toggles.
- Keep the existing hover background and inactive hover color.

## Non-goals

- Changing which filters are active or their actions.
