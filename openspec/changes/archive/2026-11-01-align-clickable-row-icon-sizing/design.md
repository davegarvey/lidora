# Design

Use 16 px Lucide icons for all clickable navigation, toolbar and row actions on desktop. Use 14 px for noninteractive status and drag indicators, and 12 px for compact inline affordances or tiny status markers, such as a removable tag's close icon or a feed error mark. Keep mobile reader action-bar icons at 16–18 px where their larger touch targets benefit from them.

Icon dimensions do not change the controls' hit areas.
