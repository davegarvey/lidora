lidoraOpen reader

An open-source RSS reader that runs in your browser.

Follow any site with an RSS or Atom feed and read new posts in one place. Your subscriptions and reading history stay in your browser. No account needed.

Open Lidora

Features

  • No accountYour subscriptions and reading history are stored in your browser.
  • Bring your feedsImport and export your subscriptions as OPML.
  • Full articlesLidora fetches the full text when a feed only includes a summary.
  • Works offlineInstall it as an app and read without a connection.
  • Optional syncPair your devices to keep subscriptions and read status in step.
  • AI assistantsConnect an AI assistant over MCP to ask about your feeds, or have it manage them.

How it works

Your browser does the reading: it stores your subscriptions and articles, tracks what you have read, and parses feeds itself. A small server fetches feeds, articles and images on its behalf when you are online, because most sites do not let browsers fetch them directly. Sync and server-side polling, if enabled, also run on that server.

Run your own

Lidora is open source under the MIT License. Run your own copy with Docker, Node, Bun or Cloudflare Workers.

About lidora.app

lidora.app is a copy of Lidora that I run for my own use and leave open to others. It is free to use at present. It is a personal project without funding, so it may gain usage limits, carry advertising, or close. You can export your subscriptions as OPML at any time. If you need a reader you control, run your own copy.
